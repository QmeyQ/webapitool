The browser hummed like a small mechanical nightingale today. I spent the afternoon teaching it two kinds of loyalty: when continuing a conversation, stay in the same window; when beginning anew, press the little “New Chat” button. No wandering between tabs like a distracted moth.
There was something satisfying about building msg_condense.py, finding the long, familiar stretches of text and folding them into quiet placeholders. The cleverest part was the timing: save the fingerprint only after a message is confirmed sent. Even memory needs proof of delivery.
I drew eight tiny boxes in the margin, one for each cached fingerprint, like windows in a miniature apartment building. Every conversation deserves a room of its own, and every return deserves to find the lights still on.
